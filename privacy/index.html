Who we are

FlowServe is a trading name of Javier Ayala, Dublin, Ireland (company in formation). Contact: javierignacioayala@gmail.com or +353 87 607 1214.

What we collect on this website

This website has no forms and sets no tracking cookies. It stores your language choice (EN/ES) in your own browser. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we keep your name, contact details and what you tell us about your business.

Why and for how long

Only to reply to you and, if you start a pilot, to deliver the service. Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months from our last contact and then deleted. Ask us and we delete them sooner.

Your clients’ data

When we run FlowServe for a business, that business is the data controller and FlowServe acts as data processor under a written agreement (GDPR Article 28). Client data is stored in the business’s own Google account (Calendar, Sheets and Gmail). We don’t sell data or use it to train AI models. If you are a client of one of those businesses, contact the business directly; we help them respond.

Providers involved

Vercel Inc. hosts this website. Depending on each business’s set-up we use Google (Workspace/Apps Script), Calendly and the business’s own payment provider. Transfers outside the EEA rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

Automated messages and AI

Messages sent by FlowServe are automated and are always sent in the business’s name. Where an AI assistant talks to people, it says so at the start and offers a human, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act. No automated decisions with legal effects are made about anyone.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing us. If you are not happy with our answer you can complain to the Data Protection Commission.
