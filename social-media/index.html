    Questions

    Do I need to give you my Instagram password?

    No. We never ask for or store passwords. Access is granted through the platforms’ own tools, such as adding us as a partner or collaborator in Meta Business Suite, which you can remove at any time.

    Who takes the photos and videos?

    You do, with your phone — we send you a simple monthly shot list. Professional photo sessions and on-site filming can be quoted separately.

    Is advertising included?

    No. The packages cover organic content. If you want to run paid ads, the ad spend is paid by you directly to the platform, and setting up campaigns is quoted separately.

    Do you guarantee followers or bookings?

    No, and be wary of anyone who does. We guarantee the agreed deliverables each month, and we review results with you so the content keeps improving.

    Can I combine it with FlowServe Front Desk?

    Yes. They are separate services with separate prices, and you can take either one or both. The plan builder above shows the combined monthly total.
