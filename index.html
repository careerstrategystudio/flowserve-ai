Questions owners ask us

Do I need to change my booking software?

No. FlowServe works with Calendly and Google Calendar today. If you use something else, tell us and we’ll check before you commit to anything.

Do my clients need to download anything?

No. Everything arrives by email from your business, and they confirm or cancel with one tap.

What happens after the 14 days?

We sit down with the numbers: no-shows before and after. If it worked, you pay a small €49 bridge rate for the rest of that month, then your plan from the 1st. If not, we switch it off and hand you back your data. No contract to break.

Is my clients’ data safe?

Your client data stays in your own Google account. We act as your data processor under a written GDPR agreement, and we don’t sell data or use it to train AI models.

Who writes the messages?

We write them with you, in your tone, and you approve them before anything goes out. They are sent from your business, not from us.
