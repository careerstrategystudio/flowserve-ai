How the 14 days go

Day 0

15-minute chat

We look at how you book today and count the last two weeks’ no-shows and late cancellations. That number is our starting point.

Days 1–3

Set-up

We connect your calendar and payment link, write the messages with you and test everything with a fake booking. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

Day 7

Check-in

A short call to adjust wording, timings or your cancellation rule.

Day 14

Results review

We compare no-shows, deposits collected and slots refilled against Day 0. You decide whether to continue.

Rest of month

Continue or switch off

Happy with it? You pay a €49 bridge rate for the rest of that calendar month. From the 1st of the next month: Essentials (€290/month) or Professional (€490/month), billed monthly, cancel any time. Not convinced? We switch it off and hand back your data — no charge.

Pilot terms

Short, plain and the same for everyone.

  1. Free for 14 days from go-live (set-up days don’t count). No payment details needed to start.
  2. Then: a €49 bridge rate for the rest of that calendar month, and from the 1st of the next month Essentials €290/month or Professional €490/month per location, billed monthly, excluding VAT. The €450 onboarding fee is waived for the first five businesses.
  3. No lock-in. Cancel with a message at any time. When you leave, we export your data to you and delete our copy within 30 days.
  4. Your money, your data. Deposits go straight to your own payment link. Client data stays in your own Google account. We sign a GDPR data processing agreement with you before go-live.
  5. You approve every message before it is sent to your clients.
  6. What we need from you: 15 minutes on Day 0, your booking link or calendar access, your payment link, and your cancellation rule.
