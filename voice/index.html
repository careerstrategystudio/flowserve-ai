No. We give you a local Irish number, and you can forward your existing line to it — always, after a few rings, or only when you’re busy.

Which calendar does it work with?

Google Calendar today. You share one calendar with us and she reads and writes only that one.

What if a caller wants a real person?

She takes the message, marks it for you in the Inbox and emails you straight away, so you can call back.

Yes, if callers are told. She says she’s an AI and that the call is transcribed at the start of every call.
